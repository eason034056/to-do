## Description

CoupleTodo helps you and your partner stay on top of life — together. Plan your days, track shared tasks, and hold each other accountable with a simple daily routine that actually sticks.

**How it works:**
- Each evening, both of you plan tomorrow's tasks before a shared cutoff time.
- Throughout the day, check off tasks as you complete them.
- At the end of the day, a daily settlement shows how each of you did.
- Miss your required tasks? A configurable penalty keeps you honest.
- Stay on track all week and unlock a shared reward you both set together.

**Core Features:**

Plan Together
Draft tomorrow's required and optional tasks before the daily cutoff. See when your partner has submitted too.

Daily Settlement
An automatic daily recap evaluates your task completion. Pass by finishing all required tasks, or face a penalty you both agreed on.

Rewards System
Set a weekly reward together — dinner out, a movie night, anything you choose. Both partners must stay on track all week to earn it.

Live Widgets & Activities
Glance at your progress from your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Live Activities show planning countdowns and settlement results in real time.

Real-Time Sync
Every task update, plan submission, and settlement syncs instantly between partners.

Notifications That Matter
Planning reminders before cutoff, settlement results, reward updates, and payment alerts — all customizable.

**Get started in seconds:**
1. Sign in and create a couple with an invite code.
2. Share the code with your partner.
3. Start planning your first day together.

CoupleTodo turns daily chores into a shared commitment. It's not just a to-do app — it's a system that helps couples follow through, together.
