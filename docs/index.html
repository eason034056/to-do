CoupleTodo

Shared Tasks & Accountability

Plan tasks together, hold each other accountable, and earn shared rewards. The daily routine that helps couples follow through.

Features

📝

Plan Together

Draft tomorrow's required and optional tasks before the daily cutoff. See when your partner has submitted too.

✅

Daily Settlement

An automatic daily recap evaluates your task completion. Pass by finishing all required tasks, or face a penalty you both agreed on.

🎁

Rewards System

Set a weekly reward together. Both partners must stay on track all week to earn it.

📱

Widgets & Live Activities

Glance at your progress from your Home Screen or Lock Screen with real-time updates.

🔃

Real-Time Sync

Every task update, plan submission, and settlement syncs instantly between partners.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Planning reminders, settlement results, reward updates, and payment alerts — all customizable.

How It Works

1

Sign in & pair up

Create your account and generate an invite code. Share it with your partner to link up.

2

Plan tomorrow's tasks

Each evening, both of you draft tasks before a shared cutoff time.

3

Complete & settle

Check off tasks during the day. At day's end, a settlement shows how you both did.

4

Earn rewards together

Stay on track all week and unlock a shared reward you both set together.
